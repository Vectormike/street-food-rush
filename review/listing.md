# Street Food Rush listing

## Short description

Cook jollof, suya, egusi, puff-puff and more for Lagos customers in 90-second shifts.

Character count: 85 (limit 120).

## Long description

Run a Lagos street-food buka for 90 seconds while danfos, kekes and okadas roll past. Customers order eleven Nigerian favourites, from jollof rice and suya to egusi soup, moi moi, dodo and pepper soup. Read the ticket, pick each ingredient from the pantry, chop the fresh ones on the board, then cook on a burner and serve before the food burns or their patience runs out. Up to three customers line up at once, and you can tap any ticket to work on that order, so juggle the burners. Burnt food goes in the bin and you cook it again. Every shift earns naira to spend in the kitchen shop on more burners, a sharper knife, faster pots, a fan, a drinks cooler and helper chefs. Eight different Lagos customers, each with 24 poses and their own temper, react with their faces and hands as they wait, from hungry to vexed, and talk back in nearly a hundred pidgin lines. Faster orders and a clean combo raise the score. A wrong ingredient, or serving before the dish is complete, costs patience and resets the combo.

The game is playable by touch at phone size. A first practice order has no clock. Pause stops the clock, the music and the sound, and sound starts only after the first tap. One optional cosmetic, the Ankara counter, can be bought through Lagos Life. It does not change the score.
